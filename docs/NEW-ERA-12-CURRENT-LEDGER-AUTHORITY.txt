NEW-ERA-12 CURRENT LEDGER AUTHORITY CONTRACT

READY proves a historical startup, not an immutable current generation.
The sealed READY anchor retains the exact WAL prefix, its current snapshot hash,
Ledger/era/retirement identities and startup authority. The active snapshot may
advance through valid INTENT/COMMIT history. Every issuance must additionally
inspect the complete current Ledger and WAL.

Trace: issueEraEntry -> assertEraAuthority(ready:true, operational:true) ->
executionEraStartup (eight activation phases, bootstrap marker, forensic and
retired evidence, active Ledger identity, Schedule era) -> durable authority
lock/epoch, Start owner and ControlledStart bindings -> canonical inspectV2Sync
of authorization.ledgerPath, its .wal and namespace staging -> historical READY
prefix binding -> operational Ledger writer binding -> grant mutation.

inspectV2 and inspectV2Sync drive the SAME inspectV2Evidence generator. There is
one set of Ledger protocol rules: complete supported WAL, seals/predecessors,
repository identity, BASE/BEGIN/INTENT/COMMIT/END linkage, monotonic generation,
snapshot/current-or-pending relationship and transaction staging. Sync evidence
reads use the existing capturedEvidence UTF8/size contract and equivalent
canonical filename and ordinary-file rules; no writes are added.

Recovery is allowed to inspect a structurally valid pending transaction or END.
Issuance is stricter on lifecycle: no pending transaction, snapshot=current,
active owner exactly equals durable Ledger lock and READY's historical writer,
and authority epoch/session equals the current live execution authority.
Terminal END has no active writer and cannot authorize a new grant. A controlled
restart establishes a successor BEGIN and new READY and may issue grants while
retaining prior consumed history.

Existing historical/read-only assertEraAuthority callers use ready:true without
operational:true. They receive complete structural inspection and historical
binding, not an implied permission to execute. completedStart uses inspectV2
and the existing shared validateEraReadyLedger historical proof. Restore,
recovery/reconciliation, runtime Ledger checks and terminalShutdown retain their
canonical inspection and phase-specific conditions. No Ledger rules are copied
into eraAdmits; legitimate grants keep their existing admission semantics.

The grant queue serializes direct duplicate issuance; ScheduleStore serializes
publication. Existing fences re-inspect current authority without caching, at
entry, after authorized, immediately before stage open, after staging, before
install/cleanup and after durable verification. The final Schedule read and authority inspection are synchronous, so no await
separates the final inspection from invoking the existing mutation primitive.
A valid transaction completed at an issuance hook is accepted after reinspection;
an invalid tail inserted at that hook is rejected. A tail invalidated after
staging prevents installation and leaves recoverable stage evidence. This does
not claim atomic protection against arbitrary out-of-process unauthorized disk
writes after the final fence; the existing exclusive Ledger/runtime ownership
contract remains required. No global lock or new durable artifact is introduced.

Mutation structure is unchanged: 14 grant + 13 Schedule publication hooks.
Canonical crash matrix remains 497 unique boundaries; NEW-ERA-12 reruns those
27 affected native abrupt-loss cases. Other 470 rows retain their prior evidence.
All mutations and fixtures remain protected OS TEMP. Real var/services are
observed only; no stage, commit or push.
