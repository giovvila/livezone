NEW-ERA-29 — execution-ownership observation and expiry ownership

API: authenticated POST /api/studio/execution-ownership with operation=inspect.
There is no separate execution-ownership CLI dispatcher in the current source.
The existing authentication/CSRF requirement and HTTP operation allowlist remain.

After the already-owned initialization promise resolves, inspect reads state and
samples the ownership clock once. snapshot(now) is a pure projection. Neither
calls expire(), matches(), acquisition, release, persistence or reconciliation.
No lease secret is returned by inspect (grant=null, leaseRemainingMs=0).

Stored active grant: effective BROWSER_OWNER, reconciliationRequired=false.
Stored expired grant (now >= expiresAt): effective NO_OWNER, reason=LEASE_EXPIRED,
reconciliationRequired=true. Effective ownerKind, ownerInstanceId and expiresAt
are null. Stored grant/principal/reason, retiredInstances and grantRevision stay
unchanged. The revision in the response is the current stored revision. This
projection does not authorize execution or prove that revocation was materialized.
No grant: current NO_OWNER/reason, reconciliationRequired=false; no placeholder.

Explicit mutating owners retain expire(): acquire/renew/release operation flow,
RestartReconciliation.current() within authenticated prepare/reconcile, and
matches() in authorized execution/publication validation. expire() materializes
LEASE_EXPIRED, clears grant/principal, retires the document identity and increments
grantRevision once. A second expiry evaluation has no additional effect. Existing
renew extends the lease without allocating a new grant incarnation/revision;
release and fresh acquisition retain their established revision behavior.

Foreign/stale submitted inspect grant fields are not authoritative and are ignored
by the existing inspect contract; inspect does not disclose another principal's
lease. Corrupt epoch evidence or conflicting authority locks fail initialization
closed (AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE). Inspection cannot repair them or break locks.
There is no persisted browser lease: only authority epoch/lock and NEW ERA startup
records persist. Restart creates a new authority process/epoch, loses the browser
grant and retains the restart fence. Startup writes belong to startup, not inspect.

The browser client requests acquire/renew/release and explicit restart challenges;
it does not use inspect to expire a lease. Diagnostic runtimeState reads snapshot;
it now obtains effective expiry without mutating the authority. No observational
caller is changed to request mutation. Existing publication validation remains
fail-closed for expired grants. No new endpoint, artifact or durable boundary.

All behavioral evidence is isolated TEMP. No real transition, proof installation,
service mutation, LIVEZONE launch, staging, commit or push. NEW-ERA-08B-C is not
resumed by this change; complete independent deployment review remains pending.
